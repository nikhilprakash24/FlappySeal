## Phase 2: Gameplay Depth (Weeks 5-8)

### 2.1 Meta-Progression System

**Unlock System**:
- 20+ seal skins (different species, colors, costumes)
- Earned through achievements
- Purchased with in-game currency
- Premium skins via IAP

**Progression Track**:
- Experience points from gameplay
- Level up system (100 levels)
- Rewards at each level
- Milestone celebrations

**Achievement System**:
- 50+ achievements across categories:
  - Distance achievements (swim 1km, 10km, 100km)
  - Score achievements (reach 10, 50, 100, 500)
  - Collection achievements (collect 100 fish, 1000 fish)
  - Challenge achievements (survive 60s without swimming up)
  - Mastery achievements (perfect run, zero collisions for 50 obstacles)
- Achievement notifications
- Progress tracking
- Social sharing

**Currency System**:
- Fish (primary currency from gameplay)
- Pearls (premium currency)
- Daily rewards
- Mission rewards

---

### 2.2 Power-Up System

**Power-Up Types** (8 core power-ups):

1. **Shield** - Survive one collision
2. **Magnet** - Auto-collect nearby fish
3. **Slow Motion** - Slow down time
4. **Speed Boost** - Move faster through water
5. **Score Multiplier** - 2x points for duration
6. **Ghost Mode** - Pass through obstacles
7. **Size Reduction** - Easier to fit through gaps
8. **Auto-Swim** - AI controls seal for brief period

**Power-Up Mechanics**:
- Random spawns in gameplay
- Can be purchased before run
- Stack multiple power-ups
- Visual/audio feedback when active
- Strategic depth in choosing when to use

---

### 2.3 Game Modes

**Endless Mode** (current, but enhanced):
- Progressive difficulty
- Biome transitions every 100 points
- Boss obstacles every 500 points

**Challenge Mode** (10+ challenges):
- Time Trial: Score 100 in 60 seconds
- No Swimming: Only dive controls work
- Narrow Gaps: Extremely tight obstacles
- Speed Run: Fastest to 50 points
- Perfect Run: 50 obstacles without error
- Vs. Ghost: Race against your best run

**Daily Challenge**:
- New challenge every day
- Leaderboards
- Special rewards
- Encourages daily engagement

**Story Mode** (stretch goal):
- 20 levels with unique objectives
- Narrative elements
- Boss fights
- Teaches advanced techniques

---

### 2.4 Boss Encounters

**Boss Types** (3-5 bosses):

1. **Giant Octopus** - Tentacles as obstacles, dodge patterns
2. **Shark** - Chase sequence, timed escape
3. **Submarine** - Nets and torpedoes
4. **Sea Monster** - Multi-phase fight
5. **Whirlpool** - Survive while being pulled

**Boss Mechanics**:
- Appear every 500 points in endless mode
- Unique patterns and challenges
- Defeat for bonus rewards
- Memorable set-piece moments
